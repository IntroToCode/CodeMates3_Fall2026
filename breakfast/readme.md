# NYC-style eggs benedict

## Description

The classic New York brunch plate: a toasted English muffin topped with seared Canadian bacon, a soft poached egg, and a generous spoonful of lemony hollandaise. New York lore credits the dish to either Delmonico's or the Waldorf Hotel in the late 1800s. Either way, it's been a Manhattan brunch staple ever since.

### Ingredients:
Hollandaise
- [ ] 3 large egg yolks
- [ ] 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- [ ] 1 tbsp water
- [ ] 1/2 cup (113 g) unsalted butter, melted and kept hot
- [ ] 1/4 tsp kosher salt
- [ ] Pinch of cayenne pepper

Benedict
- [ ] 8 large eggs, as fresh as possible
- [ ] 1 tbsp white vinegar
- [ ] 4 English muffins, split
- [ ] 8 slices Canadian bacon
- [ ] 1 tbsp butter, for the muffins and pan
- [ ] Chopped chives, paprika, and black pepper, for garnish

Coffee
- [ ] 60 g (about 3/4 cup) medium-ground coffee
- [ ] 4 cups (950 ml) water
- [ ] Milk or half-and-half and sugar, to serve (optional)

### Steps for preparation:

1. Start the coffee. Brew the ground coffee and water in a drip coffee maker (or pour-over) so it's hot and ready when the plates come together. Keep it on the warmer or in a thermal carafe.
2. Make the hollandaise. Set a heatproof bowl over a pot of barely simmering water (the bowl shouldn't touch the water). Whisk the yolks, lemon juice, and water until pale, thick, and the whisk leaves trails, about 2–3 minutes.
3. Take the bowl off the heat and drizzle in the hot melted butter very slowly, whisking constantly, until thick and glossy. Whisk in the salt and cayenne. Keep warm over the pot with the heat off; if it gets too thick, whisk in a teaspoon of warm water.
4. Set up for poaching. Fill a wide pan with about 3 inches of water, bring to a bare simmer (small bubbles, no rolling boil), and add the vinegar.
5. Toast and sear. Toast the English muffin halves and lightly butter them. In a skillet over medium heat, sear the Canadian bacon 1–2 minutes per side until lightly browned. Keep both warm in a low oven (200°F / 95°C).
6. Poach the eggs. Crack each egg into a small fine-mesh strainer to drain the watery whites, then tip it into a small cup. Stir the water gently, slide the eggs in one at a time, and cook 3 minutes for runny yolks. Work in batches of 4.
7. Lift the eggs out with a slotted spoon and blot briefly on a paper towel.
8. Assemble. Top each muffin half with a slice of Canadian bacon, then a poached egg. Spoon hollandaise over the top and finish with paprika, chives, and black pepper.
9. Serve. Plate immediately alongside mugs of hot coffee, with milk and sugar on the side.

### Notes:
1. Make-ahead eggs: Poach the eggs up to a day ahead, drop them into ice water, and refrigerate. Rewarm in hot (not simmering) water for 45–60 seconds before serving.
2. Blender hollandaise: Blend the yolks, lemon juice, water, and salt, then stream in the hot butter with the blender running. It's faster and nearly foolproof.
3. Broken hollandaise? Whisk a fresh yolk with a tablespoon of warm water in a clean bowl, then slowly whisk the broken sauce into it.
